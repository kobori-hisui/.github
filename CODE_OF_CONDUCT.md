# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in our projects to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. In short:

**Expected behavior**

- Be kind and patient, and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

**Unacceptable behavior**

- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, comments) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

Report abusive or unacceptable behavior privately to **CONTACT_EMAIL**. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers follow these steps, in order of severity: a private correction and written warning; a public or private warning with a defined period of no interaction; a temporary ban; and a permanent ban for sustained or severe violations.
